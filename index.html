User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform Terms (Term 4.b) and applicable privacy regulations, users of Light Lounge can request deletion of all personal data collected through the app and Facebook Login.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Light Lounge app and sign in.
  2. Go to My Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm. You will be asked to enter your account password.

This permanently removes your profile and booking history.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, or signed in with Facebook and have no password:

  • Email support@mytime.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - Light Lounge
  • Include your full name and the email address linked to your Facebook / Light Lounge account.

We will process the request and permanently remove your data from our active databases within 30 days.

3. Revoke Facebook permissions

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Light Lounge and click Remove.
  4. To request deletion of past platform data, open Removed Apps and Websites, select Light Lounge, and click Send Request.

See also our Privacy Policy.
