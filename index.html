1. General

We know that your privacy is important to you, and we work hard to earn and keep your trust. Light Lounge (together with its subsidiaries, “Company,” “we,” “us,” and “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it.

This Policy applies to the Light Lounge mobile app (the “App”) for iOS and Android. It describes the information we collect when you use the App, how we use, share and protect it, and how you can ask us to delete it. Information collected on our website is covered by our Website Privacy Policy.

By using the App, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. What We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect the following categories of Personal Information (information that identifies, relates to or could reasonably be linked to you or your device):

CategoryExamples
IdentifiersFirst and last name, email address, phone number, account credentials, push notification token, IP address.
Commercial informationAppointments you book, services and products purchased or considered, booking history, and payment status for visits.
Messages and contentMessages you send to a Light Lounge location through the App, and photos you choose to attach.
Geolocation dataYour device location, only if you allow it (see Device Permissions).
App activity and diagnosticsScreens viewed, actions taken, crash reports and performance data.

How we collect it

  • Directly from you, when you create an account, sign in, book or manage an appointment, message a location, or contact us.
  • From sign-in providers, if you choose to sign in with Facebook, Google or Apple (see the next section).
  • Automatically, through the App and trusted service providers that provide analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring and push notifications (such as Google Firebase).

Please provide only relevant information and do not send unnecessary sensitive information, such as Social Security numbers, in free-text fields or messages.

3. Facebook Login and Other Sign-In Options

You may choose to sign in to the App using Facebook Login, Google or Apple. If you use Facebook Login, Facebook shares with us the basic profile information you authorize, such as your name and email address. We use it only to create and secure your Light Lounge account and to let you book and manage appointments. We do not post to your Facebook account, and we do not receive your Facebook password.

Facebook’s handling of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. You can stop sharing data with us at any time by removing Light Lounge in your Facebook settings (see Data Deletion Instructions).

4. Device Permissions

The App asks for the following permissions. Each is optional and you can change it at any time in your device settings, though some features may not work without it.

  • Location: to automatically check you in for your appointment when you arrive at the business.
  • Camera and photos: to take or attach a photo in a message.
  • Calendar: to add your appointment to your calendar.
  • Notifications: to send appointment reminders, messages from your location and other updates you request.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing the information, appointments, products and services you request;
  • Creating and securing your account, including security and fraud prevention;
  • Providing effective customer service and responding to your messages;
  • Sending appointment confirmations, reminders and notices related to your use of the App;
  • Sending special offers and other information, in accordance with your preferences;
  • Improving the content, functionality, performance and stability of the App and our services.

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or lease your Personal Information to any third party. We may disclose it for a business purpose to:

  • Our affiliates; and
  • Service providers that help us operate the App and our services, such as appointment and booking software, payment processing, analytics and crash reporting, push notifications, and customer support. These providers are bound by contract to keep your information confidential and use it only for the services they provide to us.

We may also share information to comply with law or legal process, respond to authorized government requests, enforce our agreements, investigate and prevent security threats or fraud, protect someone’s safety, or in connection with a sale, merger or reorganization of our business. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Your Choices

  • Permissions and notifications: change or revoke them in your device settings.
  • Marketing: we send marketing emails only if you consent, and you can opt out with the “unsubscribe” link. For text messages, reply STOP to opt out. Transactional messages, such as appointment confirmations, are not affected.
  • Access and correction: you can view and update much of your information in the App under My Account, or contact us for help.
  • Deletion: see Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Data Retention

We keep Personal Information for as long as reasonably necessary to carry out the business purpose for which it was collected, including providing the App and your appointments, meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes and enforcing our agreements. When you delete your account or send us a verified deletion request, we remove your Personal Information as described below, except for information we are required by law to keep.

9. Data Deletion Instructions

You have the right to request deletion of the personal data we hold about you, including data collected through the App and Facebook Login. You can do this in any of the three ways below.

A. Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the Light Lounge app and sign in.
  2. Go to My Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm. You will be asked to enter your account password.

This permanently removes your profile and booking history.

B. Request deletion through our Contact Us form

If you cannot access the App, or signed in with Facebook and do not have a password:

  • Open the Light Lounge Contact Us form (lightlounge.life/contact).
  • Start your message with: Data Deletion Request - Light Lounge
  • Enter your full name, and the email address and phone number linked to your Facebook / Light Lounge account, so we can find and verify your account.
  • You can also call us at 720-791-3000.

We will process the request and permanently remove your data from our active databases within 30 days.

C. Remove Light Lounge from your Facebook account

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Light Lounge and click Remove.
  4. To request deletion of past platform data, open Removed Apps and Websites, select Light Lounge, and click Send Request.

10. Information of Minors

We do not intentionally seek to gather information from individuals under the age of eighteen (18), and the App is not directed to minors. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Information about a minor, we will take steps to securely remove it from our systems.

11. Safeguarding Your Information

We use reasonable technical, administrative and physical safeguards to protect your Personal Information against accidental loss and unauthorized access, use, alteration and disclosure. However, we can never promise 100% security. If you believe your information has been compromised or is at risk, please contact us.
